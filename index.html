trending_down Professional Notes:
  • • SWR (Safe Withdrawal Rate): The 4% rule is a conservative benchmark. Rates <4% = safer, >5% = higher risk of portfolio depletion.
  • • Pension Access: UK pensions unlock at age 57 (age 55 for those who reached the age before 6 April 2023). Plan your drawdown accordingly.
  • • Tax Efficiency: The Tax & Healthcare Drag buffer accounts for ISA withdrawal efficiency but real plans should model capital gains tax, dividend allowances, and healthcare costs.
  • • Mortgage Real Terms: Your mortgage gets progressively cheaper in real terms. By decumulation, a fixed payment is genuinely manageable.
  • • Monte Carlo Testing: Stress-test your plan against 200 simulated market paths to measure sequence-of-returns risk (SORR).
