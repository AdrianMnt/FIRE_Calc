Model assumptions

One person, one defined-contribution pension and one fixed-rate mortgage. Part-time and retirement income tax uses annualised 2026/27 rates in today’s pounds. Tax bands rise with inflation in the projection. The remaining tax-free pension allowance is a fixed nominal limit.

Work and Coast budgets use the take-home pay you enter, including any State Pension already included in your budget. Coast can use its own take-home income. The separate State Pension forecast is paid and taxed during part-time work and full retirement.

Cash earns 0% nominal interest. Home value and other assets and debts stay flat in real terms and count towards net worth. The model assumes no sale of these assets or additional cash flows from them. Include other debt costs in your spending assumptions.

Each scenario must cover every month to the horizon and clear the mortgage by then. The safety buffer increases the spending used to test eligibility. The projection and stress test use your stated spending.

Verify your State Pension forecast and private pension access age. The normal minimum pension age rises from 55 to 57 on 6 April 2028. Enter a scheme-specific age where applicable.
